<Info>
**The core idea:** We settle tokens and balances on-chain, but run strategy execution and data reporting off-chain. Every off-chain component has a specific practical reason for being off-chain and a defined protection mechanism.
</Info>

**Prerequisites:** [What is ArcX?](/learn/protocol-overview), [vToken](/learn/vtoken)

## What's On-Chain

These components live on Starknet. You can verify them directly.

| Component | What You Can Verify |
|---|---|
| **vToken** | Balances, transfers, total supply, NAV per share |
| **ST token** | Balances, transfers, total supply per epoch |
| **EPT token** | Balances, transfers, accumulated credit balances |
| **PointsToken** | Total supply, burns, per-address balances |
| **Credit computation** | GCI derived from NAV — fully deterministic, no oracle |
| **Settlement** | Every deposit, order match, and redemption executes as an on-chain transaction |

<Info>
Credits are computed on-chain from the vault's NAV using the GCI formula. This is a significant trust improvement over the previous model, which required an off-chain credits oracle. There is no credits oracle in the current architecture.
</Info>

ArcX does not hold your tokens. vToken, ST, EPT, and PointsToken are standard tokens on Starknet. Your balances are yours. Our oracles feed *data* into the contracts (NAV, total points), but the contracts enforce the math.

## What's Off-Chain (and Why)

Four components run off-chain. Each one is off-chain for a specific practical reason.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Matching Engine" icon="gears">
    **What it does:** Matches buy and sell orders on the ST/vToken and EPT/vToken orderbook. Handles splitting vTokens when buy orders on both sides match, and recombining when sell orders match.

    **Why it's off-chain:** Orderbook matching requires iterating over all open orders — too expensive on-chain. Same architecture as other performant DEXes.

    **The pattern:** Off-chain matching, on-chain settlement.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Strategy Execution" icon="chart-mixed">
    **What it does:** The market maker borrows vault capital and runs trading strategies on perp DEXes.

    **Why it's off-chain:** The exchanges themselves are off-chain. You can't execute trades on external DEXes from a Starknet smart contract.

    **The pattern:** Market maker borrows capital, pays a fixed interest rate. The vault's NAV reflects the fixed yield, not variable trading PnL.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="NAV Oracle" icon="dollar-sign">
    **What it does:** Reports the total USDC value of the vault. NAV determines how many vToken shares you receive on deposit and how much USDC you receive on withdrawal.

    **Why it's off-chain:** NAV requires reading the vault's position across external exchanges. No third-party oracle can access this data.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Points Oracle" icon="star">
    **What it does:** Reports total points earned by the strategy at each weekly distribution. This number determines the points-per-credit conversion for all holders.

    **Why it's off-chain:** Exchange points are tracked per-account on the exchange platform. They are not public on-chain data.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## How Each Off-Chain Component Is Protected

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Matching Engine" icon="gears">
    **Protection:** Settlement is on-chain. The engine determines *which* orders fill, but actual token transfers execute through smart contracts. A matching engine bug could cause missed fills, but cannot move tokens without on-chain settlement.

    **Verification:** Every matched trade produces an on-chain settlement transaction.

    **Bounding:** If the engine goes down, no trades execute, but funds are safe. Worst case is missed trading opportunities.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Strategy Execution" icon="chart-mixed">
    **Protection:** The market maker pays a fixed interest rate regardless of their trading performance. The vault's yield is contractually defined, not dependent on variable PnL.

    **Verification:** NAV updates reflect the fixed yield. Track the NAV trajectory throughout the epoch.

    **Bounding:** Epoch isolation prevents compounding. A bad epoch does not automatically roll your capital into the next one.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="NAV Oracle" icon="dollar-sign">
    **Protection:** Deposits revert if NAV data is stale. The contract enforces a maximum staleness window.

    **Verification:** NAV trajectory over an epoch should be consistent with the fixed interest rate.

    **Bounding:** Each epoch is independent. A single stale update does not compound.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Points Oracle" icon="star">
    **Protection:** Off-chain safeguards ensure reported points match what the strategy actually earned.

    **Verification:** You can use heuristics and compare reported points against broader market standards.

    **Bounding:** Final points determine the conversion ratio for all holders.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
