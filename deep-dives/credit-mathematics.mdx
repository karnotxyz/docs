Credits determine your share of points. Points are distributed weekly, not as a single lump sum at epoch end. This page explains how credits accumulate and how weekly distribution rewards early holders.

**Prerequisites:** [What is ArcX?](/learn/protocol-overview), [vToken](/learn/vtoken)

---

## Why Credits Exist

Without credits, points distribution would be trivially gameable. A naive design that distributes points proportional to token balance at epoch end lets anyone deposit one second before maturity and claim the same share as a day-one holder.

Credits prevent this by tracking **how much you held** and **for how long**. Your share of points is proportional to your cumulative credit balance, not a single end-of-epoch snapshot. A last-second depositor accumulates nearly zero credits and receives a negligible share of points.

---

## How Credits Accumulate

Credits are computed on-chain from the vault's NAV using the **Global Credit Index (GCI)**. No oracle. Fully deterministic.

### The GCI Formula

The GCI tracks credits earned per share. For any time interval:

$$\text{GCI increment} = \frac{\text{NAV}_{start} + \text{NAV}_{end}}{2} \times \text{days}$$

This is the average NAV over the interval multiplied by the duration. Geometrically, it's the area of a trapezoid: because NAV grows linearly (fixed-rate yield), the capital curve is a straight line, and the area under it equals the credits.

Equivalently:

$$\text{GCI increment} = r_1 \times \Delta t + \frac{1}{2} \times \Delta t \times (r_2 - r_1)$$

where $r_1$ = NAV at start, $r_2$ = NAV at end, $\Delta t$ = duration in days.

### Your Credits

Your credits for any interval are your share balance times the GCI increment:

$$\text{your credits} = \text{your balance} \times \text{GCI increment}$$

Where balance = your vToken holdings + your EPT holdings. Both earn credits at the same rate — 1 vToken = 1 EPT for credit purposes.

Credits accumulate across the epoch. They are tracked per weekly period for distribution.

## Fresh Credits on Transfer

When tokens change hands — whether through the orderbook or through a transfer — the new holder's credit counter starts from zero at the moment of acquisition.

- **Orderbook purchase:** You buy EPT. You start with zero credits. The seller retains all credits they accrued.
- **Minting:** You deposit USDC and receive freshly minted vTokens. They start at zero credits.
- **No credit inheritance.** Credits are non-transferable. The buyer never receives the seller's accumulated credits.

---

## Weekly Points Distribution

Points are not distributed as a single lump sum at epoch end. Each week's points are distributed separately among that week's credit holders:

$$\text{yourPoints} = \sum_{w=1}^{W} \frac{\text{yourCredits}_w}{\text{totalCredits}_w} \times \text{points}_w$$

where $W$ is the number of weeks, $\text{yourCredits}_w$ is your credits during week $w$, $\text{totalCredits}_w$ is the sum of all holders' credits for that week, and $\text{points}_w$ is the points earned during that week.

### Why Weekly Matters

Weekly distribution rewards early holders. When the platform is new and few people hold tokens, the denominator ($\text{totalCredits}_w$) is small. Early holders get a larger share of those early weeks' points. As more holders join in later weeks, the denominator grows — but the early weeks are already locked in.

## Worked Examples

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Early-Holder Advantage" icon="users">
    **Setup:** 10-week epoch. Strategy earns 500 points per week (5,000 total).
    - Alice holds 100 vTokens from week 1
    - Bob buys 100 EPT at week 7

    **Weekly distribution:**

    | Week | Alice credits | Bob credits | Total credits | Alice's share | Bob's share |
    |------|-------------|------------|--------------|--------------|------------|
    | 1-6  | 100 each week | 0 | 100 | 100% | 0% |
    | 7-10 | 100 each week | 100 each week | 200 | 50% | 50% |

    **Points:**

    | Holder | Weeks 1-6 | Weeks 7-10 | Total | Share |
    |--------|-----------|------------|-------|-------|
    | Alice  | 3,000 (100%) | 1,000 (50%) | **4,000** | **80%** |
    | Bob    | 0 | 1,000 (50%) | **1,000** | **20%** |

    Alice gets 80% of total points even though Bob held the same amount for 40% of the epoch. The early weeks — when Alice was the sole holder — are locked in at 100%.
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Late Entrants" icon="chart-mixed">
    **Setup:** 10-week epoch. Strategy earns 500 points per week (5,000 total).
    - Alice holds 100 vTokens from week 1
    - 9 other holders each buy 100 EPT at week 6

    **Weekly distribution:**

    | Period | Alice credits | Others (total) | Alice's share of that period |
    |--------|-------------|---------------|---------------------------|
    | Weeks 1-5 | 100 | 0 | 100% |
    | Weeks 6-10 | 100 | 900 | 10% |

    **Points:**

    | Holder | Points |
    |--------|--------|
    | Alice  | 2,500 (weeks 1-5 at 100%) + 250 (weeks 6-10 at 10%) = **2,750** |
    | Each of 9 others | 250 each |

    Alice earns **55%** of all points despite holding only 10% of total tokens from week 6 onward. She captured the early weeks when participation was low.
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Last-Second Attack" icon="clock">
    **Setup:** 10-week epoch. Strategy earns 500 points per week (5,000 total).
    - Alice holds 100 vTokens for all 10 weeks
    - Eve buys 100 EPT in the final week

    **Points:**

    | Holder | Points | Share |
    |--------|--------|-------|
    | Alice  | 4,500 + 250 = **4,750** | **95%** |
    | Eve    | 250 | **5%** |

    Eve deposited the same dollar amount as Alice but only participates in 1 out of 10 weekly distributions. The attack is economically neutralized.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Info>
These examples use simplified credit amounts for clarity. In production, credit amounts vary with NAV growth — the GCI formula accounts for this automatically.
