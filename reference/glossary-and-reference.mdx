## Tokens

| Token | What it is | Tradeable | Redeemable for | Scope |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **vToken** | Base vault share. Earns yield + points. | Yes | USDC + PointsTokens at maturity | Per vault, per epoch |
| **ST** | Yield component of vToken | Yes (orderbook) | USDC at final NAV | Per vault, per epoch |
| **EPT** | Points component of vToken | Yes (orderbook) | PointsTokens at maturity | Per vault, per epoch |
| **PointsToken** | 1:1 exchange points | Yes | Airdrop tokens after TGE | Per exchange, persists |

### Split Equation

$$1 \text{ vToken} = 1 \text{ ST} + 1 \text{ EPT}$$

The matching engine enforces this parity internally when orders match.

---

## Key Equations

| Equation | Meaning |
|---|---|
| shares = deposit / NAV | vToken shares minted on deposit |
| NAV = vault value / total shares | Per-share USDC value of the vault |
| 1 vToken = 1 ST + 1 EPT | Split parity (bidirectional) |
| ST price + EPT price = vToken price | Pricing parity |
| your points = Σ (your credits_w / total credits_w) × points_w | Weekly points distribution by credit share |

### GCI (Global Credit Index)

Credits per share per interval, computed on-chain from NAV:

$$\text{GCI increment} = \frac{\text{NAV}_{start} + \text{NAV}_{end}}{2} \times \text{days}$$

$$\text{your credits} = \text{your shares} \times \text{GCI increment}$$

Both vToken and EPT holders earn credits at the same rate. No oracle.

## Fees

| Fee | Rate | Applied |
|---|---|---|
| Performance fee (yield) | 20% of interest earned | On yield accrual |
| PointsToken claim fee | 25% of PointsTokens | On claiming PointsTokens |

---

## Epoch State Machine

| State | Deposits | Credit Accrual | Orderbook Trading | Redemption |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **ACTIVE** | Yes | Yes | Yes | No |
| **SETTLING** | No | Stopped | Secondary only | No |
| **MATURED** | No | Final | No | Yes |

At maturity: ST redeems for USDC. EPT claims PointsTokens. vToken holders can claim both directly.

---

## Withdrawal

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Request** | Shares burned. Yield and credit accrual stops. USDC owed locked at current NAV. |
| **Finalize** | After up to 3 days, USDC released. Delay covers on-chain/exchange settlement. |

If you hold ST + EPT, recombine to vToken first.
