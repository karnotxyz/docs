## The Problem

Points farming on perp DEXes is capital-inefficient. You deposit capital, lock it in a strategy, earn points alongside yield, and hope for an airdrop. You can't sell the points pre-TGE, you can't separate the yield from the points, and you can't get leveraged exposure to either one independently.

## The Solution

We wrap perp DEX strategies into a **vToken** — a vault share that earns both fixed yield and exchange points. Hold it for the combined return, or use your vTokens to buy **ST** (yield) or **EPT** (points) on the orderbook.

Points farmers buy EPT for leveraged points exposure. Yield seekers buy ST at a discount for fixed returns. Each user gets exactly the exposure they want, at a price set by the market.

## How It Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Deposit USDC">
    Deposit USDC into a vault. You receive **vToken** shares at the current NAV. Your vToken earns fixed yield and accrues credits for points. You can stop here.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Trade (optional)">
    Use your vTokens to buy **EPT** (points leverage) or **ST** (discounted yield) on the orderbook. One orderbook, two pairs: ST/vToken and EPT/vToken. The matching engine handles the rest.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Earn">
    **Yield:** The market maker borrows vault capital and pays a fixed interest rate (e.g. 15% APR gross, 12% net after performance fee). NAV grows linearly.

    **Points:** Both vToken and EPT holders accrue credits — a time-weighted measure of your position. Each week, exchange points are distributed proportional to your credit share. Earlier holders earn more. [Credit Mathematics →](/deep-dives/credit-mathematics)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Redeem">
    At epoch maturity: ST redeems for USDC at final NAV. EPT claims PointsTokens. vToken holders can do either.

    To withdraw before maturity: sell on the orderbook, recombine to vToken if needed, then request withdrawal. Finalization takes up to 3 days.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Pricing

Since 1 vToken = 1 ST + 1 EPT, their prices are locked together:

$$\text{ST price} + \text{EPT price} = \text{vToken price}$$

If prices drift, arbitrageurs can trade to capture the gap, keeping pricing tight. [EPT Pricing →](/deep-dives/ept-pricing)

## Epochs

Each strategy runs in epochs that progress through three stages:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Active">
    <Badge color="green">ACTIVE</Badge>

    Deposits open. Credits accruing. Orderbooks open. Splitting and recombining available.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Settling">
    <Badge color="orange">SETTLING</Badge>

    Strategy unwinds. No new deposits. Orderbooks open for secondary trades only.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Matured">
    <Badge color="red">MATURED</Badge>

    Final NAV and points published. ST redeems for USDC. EPT claims PointsTokens.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Epochs are independent. No automatic rollover. See [Epoch Lifecycle](/learn/epoch-lifecycle) for details.

## Vaults at Launch

Each vault pairs a professional market maker with one or more perp DEXes. The market maker borrows vault capital, runs their own strategies (market making, arbitrage, etc.), and pays back a fixed interest rate. ArcX does not dictate or operate the trading — each market maker runs their own book.

Each vault has its own vToken. Splitting produces ST and EPT specific to that vault and epoch.
