## What Is a vToken

A vToken is a vault share. Each vault pairs a market maker with one or more perp DEXes. You deposit USDC, receive vToken shares, and earn two things:

1. **Fixed yield** — the market maker borrows your capital and pays a fixed interest rate
2. **Exchange points** — the strategy's open interest generates points, distributed to you based on how much you held and for how long

One vToken per vault. Different vaults have different market makers, exchanges, and rates.

<Info>
vToken is the simplest way to use ArcX. You earn yield and points without splitting, trading, or managing anything. Just deposit and hold.
</Info>

---

## Deposit

Deposit USDC into a vault. You receive vToken shares at the current <Tooltip tip="Net Asset Value. Per-share USDC value of the vault.">NAV</Tooltip>:

$$\text{shares} = \frac{\text{deposit amount}}{\text{NAV}}$$

First deposit into an empty vault mints shares 1:1 with USDC. After that, shares are priced by NAV.

---

## Yield

Yield comes from a **fixed-rate loan**. The market maker borrows vault capital and pays back a fixed annual rate. NAV grows linearly at that rate.

| Term | Example |
|---|---|
| Gross APR | 15% |
| Performance fee | 20% of returns |
| Net APR | 12% |

NAV only reflects hard assets (USDC value). It does not price in points — points are tracked separately through credits.

---

## Credits and Points

Your vToken accrues **credits** while you hold it — the longer you hold and the more you hold, the bigger your share of points. Credits are tracked automatically on-chain from the vault's NAV. No oracle, nothing to claim manually.

Each week, the exchange delivers points. Those points are split among credit holders proportional to their share. Hold earlier, earn more — early weeks are locked in and can't be diluted by later depositors.

If you buy vTokens on secondary or receive them via transfer, your credit counter starts from zero. The previous holder keeps their credits.

See [Credit Mathematics](/deep-dives/credit-mathematics) for the formula.

## The Split

You can split your vTokens into their two components at any time:

$$1 \text{ vToken} = 1 \text{ ST} + 1 \text{ EPT}$$

- **ST (Strategy Token)** — the yield claim. Redeems for USDC at NAV after maturity.
- **EPT (Expected Points Token)** — the points claim. Accrues credits. Claims PointsTokens after maturity.

This is **bidirectional**. Recombine 1 ST + 1 EPT back into 1 vToken at any time. No fee on splitting or recombining — fees only apply on orderbook matches.

### Why Split?

Splitting lets you concentrate into the side you want.

**Points leverage:** Split 100 vTokens into 100 ST + 100 EPT. Sell the 100 ST for \~\$98 worth of vTokens. Buy EPT at \$0.02 each — that's 4,900 more EPT. You now hold **5,000 EPT** from a \$100 deposit. 50x points exposure.

**Cheaper yield:** Split and sell your EPT. The proceeds effectively lower your cost basis on the ST you kept. Points farmers buying EPT push ST prices down — you pick up ST at a discount and earn the full fixed-rate yield on top.

If you don't have a preference, don't split. Holding vToken gives you both.

---

## Withdrawal

Withdrawals are two-phase:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Request">
    Your shares are burned immediately. You stop earning yield and credits. The USDC you're owed is locked at current NAV.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finalize">
    After a short delay (up to 3 days), your USDC is released.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If you hold ST and EPT instead of vToken, recombine them first, then withdraw.

---

## At Maturity

When the epoch matures, vToken holders can claim directly — no splitting required:

- **USDC** at the final NAV (the yield you earned)
- **PointsTokens** based on your accumulated credit share (the points you earned)

There is no deadline on redemption.

If you want to claim only one side (just the USDC, or just the points), split your vToken into ST and EPT first, then redeem each independently.
