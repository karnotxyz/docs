## Quick Mapping

| Pendle | ArcX | Key Difference |
|---|---|---|
| **SY** (Standardized Yield) | **vToken** | Both are the yield-bearing wrapper before the split. Pendle wraps external yield sources. ArcX's vToken IS the vault — it earns yield and points directly. |
| **PT** (Principal Token) | **ST** (Strategy Token) | Both represent a claim on principal. PT redeems at par. ST redeems at NAV (which grows with fixed-rate yield). |
| **YT** (Yield Token) | **EPT** (Expected Points Token) | Both isolate the variable side. YT accrues yield. EPT accrues credits for a points claim. |
| Pendle AMM (PT &lt;&gt; SY) | ST/vToken + EPT/vToken orderbook | Pendle uses one AMM. ArcX uses one orderbook with two pairs, denominated in vTokens. |
| Two-way arbitrage (mint + redeem) | Two-way arbitrage (split + recombine) | Both have tight pricing corridors because both directions are enforceable. |

---

## The Big Difference: Market Structure

Pendle routes everything through a single AMM between PT and SY. ArcX runs one orderbook with two pairs (ST/vToken and EPT/vToken). When buy orders on both pairs match, the engine splits vTokens and delivers each side. When sell orders match, the engine recombines.

Both systems split deposits into a principal claim and a variable claim. Both have the same parity. Pendle: PT + YT = SY. ArcX: ST + EPT = vToken.

The other key difference: Pendle wraps on-chain yield-bearing assets. ArcX's vToken IS the yield-bearing asset — the vault lends capital to a market maker who pays a fixed rate. Pendle doesn't execute strategies. ArcX's vaults do (through market makers).

## When to Use Which

| If you want to... | Pendle | ArcX |
|---|---|---|
| Earn fixed yield on a yield-bearing asset | Buy PT at a discount | Buy ST at a discount, or hold vToken |
| Get leveraged yield/points exposure | Buy YT | Buy EPT on the orderbook |
| Get leveraged pre-TGE points exposure | Indirect (YT on point-earning assets) | Buy EPT on the orderbook |
| Separate strategy PnL from points | Not possible | Buy ST or EPT independently |
| Earn both yield and points with no splitting | Hold SY | Hold vToken |

Pendle is built for on-chain yield optimization. ArcX is built for perp DEX strategy outcomes: fixed yield and pre-TGE points. You could reasonably use both.
