EPT is the points component of a [vToken](/learn/vtoken). Buy EPT on the orderbook with your vTokens to isolate the points side.

## What Is EPT

EPT is your claim on exchange points earned by the vault's strategy during the epoch. While you hold EPT, you accrue credits. At maturity, your credit share determines how many PointsTokens you receive.

EPT trades on the **EPT/vToken** orderbook. Points farmers buy EPT for leveraged exposure — at \$0.02 per EPT, 100 vTokens buys 5,000 EPT instead of holding 100 vTokens. That's 50x points leverage.

---

## Credits

EPT accrues **credits** while you hold it — the longer you hold and the more you hold, the bigger your share of points. 1 EPT earns credits at the same rate as 1 vToken. Credits are tracked automatically on-chain from the vault's NAV. No oracle.

**Fresh start.** When you buy EPT, your credit counter starts from zero. The seller keeps their accumulated credits. No credit inheritance.

**Early-holder advantage.** Points are distributed weekly. In early weeks when fewer people hold EPT, each holder gets a larger share. Those early weeks are locked in — later entrants can't dilute them.

See [Credit Mathematics](/deep-dives/credit-mathematics) for the GCI formula and worked examples.

## Pricing

Since 1 vToken = 1 ST + 1 EPT, EPT price is linked to ST:

$$\text{EPT price} = \text{vToken price} - \text{ST price}$$

If NAV is 1.00 and ST trades at \$0.98, EPT fair value is \$0.02. The matching engine keeps pricing tight. See [EPT Pricing](/deep-dives/ept-pricing) for the full treatment.

### EPT Price Falls Over the Epoch

As the epoch progresses, fewer credits remain to be earned. An EPT bought at week 1 accrues credits for many more weeks than one bought at week 6. The market prices this in: EPT gets cheaper over time.

Same price at different times means different APR. The UI shows both.

---

## Fees

A **25% claim fee** is charged when claiming PointsTokens after maturity. A **20% performance fee** applies to yield generated by the vault.

No fee on EPT redemption.

---

## Claiming PointsTokens

After the epoch matures, claim your share as [PointsTokens](/learn/points-token). Your allocation is based on your credit share. PointsTokens are minted to your wallet.

Each epoch produces a separate EPT. Credits do not carry over between epochs.
