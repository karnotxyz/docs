Everything starts with depositing USDC for vTokens. From there, you choose your path.

---

## Deposit

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select a vault">
    Each vault pairs a market maker with one or more exchanges. Different vaults have different rates and point sources.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deposit USDC">
    Enter your amount. You receive vToken shares at the current NAV.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Done">
    Your vTokens earn fixed yield (NAV grows) and accrue credits for points. You can stop here — this is the simplest path.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## Buy EPT or ST

If you want isolated exposure, use your vTokens to buy on the orderbook.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Points Farmer" icon="chart-line">
    You want leveraged points exposure.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open the EPT orderbook">
        Navigate to the EPT orderbook for the vault you're in.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Place a buy order">
        Set your APR and quantity. You're paying with vTokens. At \$0.02 per EPT, 100 vTokens buys 5,000 EPT — 50x points leverage. The UI shows both APR and leverage.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Order fills">
        Your order matches and EPT lands in your wallet.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Hold and earn">
        Credits accrue automatically while you hold EPT. No action needed. Earlier holders earn more — your early weeks are locked in.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Claim after maturity">
        Epoch matures. Claim your PointsTokens based on your credit share.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Yield Seeker" icon="vault">
    You want fixed USDC returns.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open the ST orderbook">
        Navigate to the ST orderbook for the vault you're in.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Place a buy order">
        Buy ST below NAV with your vTokens. The vault pays 12% net APR. The discount on top is your bonus.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Order fills">
        Your order matches and ST lands in your wallet.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Hold through the epoch">
        ST tracks the vault's NAV. NAV grows at the fixed rate. Monitor on the dashboard.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Redeem after maturity">
        Epoch matures. Redeem ST for USDC at the final NAV.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

---

## Exiting Before Maturity

You don't have to wait. Sell your tokens on the orderbook at any time during the active epoch.

- **Sell EPT** → receive vTokens. Seller keeps all accrued credits. Buyer starts from zero.
- **Sell ST** → receive vTokens.

To convert vTokens back to USDC, request a withdrawal (see below).

---

## Withdrawal

Withdrawals convert vTokens to USDC in two steps:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Request">
    Your vToken shares are burned. You stop earning yield and credits. The USDC you're owed is locked at current NAV.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finalize">
    After a short delay (up to 3 days), your USDC is released. The delay covers on-chain/exchange settlement.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**If you hold ST or EPT**, sell them on the orderbook first to receive vTokens, then withdraw. Or recombine ST + EPT into vTokens if you hold both.

---

## After Maturity

When an epoch reaches **MATURED**, final NAV and total points are locked in. No deadline to redeem — do it whenever you're ready.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="vToken Holders" icon="wallet">
    Claim directly:
    - **USDC** at the final NAV
    - **PointsTokens** based on your credit share
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="ST Holders" icon="vault">
    Redeem ST for USDC at the final NAV. Your ST is burned.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="EPT Holders" icon="star">
    Claim PointsTokens based on your accumulated credit share. A 25% claim fee is deducted from your PointsTokens.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

### What to Do with PointsTokens

PointsTokens represent actual exchange points. Hold them until the exchange conducts its TGE, then burn your PointsTokens to receive airdrop tokens. See [PointsToken](/learn/points-token) for details.

## Quick Reference

| Action | <Badge color="green">ACTIVE</Badge> | <Badge color="orange">SETTLING</Badge> | <Badge color="red">MATURED</Badge> |
|---|---|---|---|
| Deposit USDC for vTokens | Yes | No | No |
| Buy/sell EPT on orderbook | Yes | Secondary only | No |
| Buy/sell ST on orderbook | Yes | Secondary only | No |
| Credits accruing | Yes | Stopped | Final |
| Withdraw vTokens | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Redeem ST for USDC | No | No | **Yes** |
| Claim EPT for PointsTokens | No | No | **Yes** |
| Claim vToken (both) | No | No | **Yes** |

---

## Fees

| Fee | Rate | When |
|---|---|---|
| Performance fee (yield) | 20% of interest earned | On yield accrual |
| PointsToken claim fee | 25% of PointsTokens | On claiming PointsTokens |
